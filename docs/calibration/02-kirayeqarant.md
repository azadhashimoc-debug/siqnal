# Calibration example 02: "KirayəQarant" (broker-free, safe apartment rental) (written by the user)

## Problem
Rental housing in Baku is where students and families get cheated most often.
- Fake brokers post attractive photos and collect a 50–100 AZN "viewing" or deposit fee. Then they block the number.
- Landlord and tenant have no formal contract. The landlord can evict the tenant at any time. The tenant can damage the flat and leave without paying utilities.

## Solution
- A listings platform with listings directly from landlords only. The landlord's ownership extract (kupça) and identity are verified with Asan İmza or FIN code.
- Escrow that holds a 1-month deposit, plus generation of a standard legal rental contract.

## Monetization
Brokers take a one-time 20–30%. The platform charges both sides a 5–10% service fee and insures both of them.

## What this example adds (new lessons)
1. **Trust and fraud problems count as a separate category.** A problem with no money loss but a risk of being cheated is a strong signal.
2. **The pain is two-sided.** Both landlord and tenant suffer, so the platform can charge both.
3. **The price is positioned against the incumbent.** The platform is cheaper than the broker's 20–30%.
4. **The idea uses state infrastructure** (Asan İmza, FIN) as a trust layer.

## What the agent must add
- **Licensing:** escrow and insurance may need a license from the Central Bank of Azerbaijan. Check this requirement.
- **API access:** is there real API access to Asan İmza or the property registry for third parties?
- **Competition:** bina.az, tap.az and similar platforms. Do they already verify owners?
- **Evidence:** fraud complaints (news, Facebook groups, forums) and their frequency.
- **Numbers:** verify the 50–100 AZN fee and the 20–30% broker commission with links.
